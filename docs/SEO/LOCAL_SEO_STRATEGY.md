## Local entity consistency
Use one source of truth for clinic name, address, phone, WhatsApp, coordinates and opening hours. The values already present in the site are not independently verified by this audit. Confirm all of them with the clinic owner before launch.

## Linking
Link from service descriptions to genuinely relevant patient education pages; link articles back to the relevant service page and doctor profile. Avoid generic repeated anchor text and excessive exact-match anchors.

## Reputation and citations
Claim/verify the genuine Google Business Profile, ensure NAP consistency, add genuine clinic photographs and accurate service descriptions, and request honest feedback without incentives or review gating. Do not create duplicate profiles or fabricate citations.
